CHAPTER 3
Agents vs Chatbots
Chatbots respond to questions with text. They are reactive — you ask, they answer, you do the work.
AI Agents are proactive — given a goal, they plan the steps needed, use tools to execute each step, check the results, and complete the whole task without you doing anything else.
What agents can do: Access databases, send emails, book calendars, run code, browse the web, call APIs, coordinate with other agents — all in service of completing your goal.
The key word is autonomy. Agents operate with enough independence to complete multi-step tasks the way a competent colleague would.

Multi-Agent Orchestration
Multi-agent systems allow specialised agents to work together on complex tasks — each handling what it does best while coordinating automatically.
Orchestrator agents manage other agents — assigning tasks, collecting results, and making decisions about next steps, like a project manager coordinating a team.
Why it matters: Complex business processes involve many steps and systems. Multi-agent systems mirror real organisational workflows, making automation of whole processes possible.
Gemini Enterprise provides the infrastructure to build, deploy, and monitor these multi-agent systems with full visibility into what each agent is doing.

CHAPTER 8
Governance and Safety
Agent Registry: A central catalogue of all agents in your organisation — what they do, who built them, what permissions they have. No shadow agents operating unchecked.
Agent Gateway: Every agent action passes through the gateway, which enforces policies — blocking unauthorised data access, flagging unusual behaviour, logging everything.
Audit logs: A complete record of every action every agent takes — essential for compliance, security investigations, and building organisational trust in AI systems.
Human oversight: For high-stakes decisions, agents can be configured to pause and request human approval before proceeding. AI autonomy with appropriate human control.
