## About

**What is Gemini Enterprise!** is a three-volume interactive flippable comic series on Googlr Enterprise — Your AI Teammate at Work — How Google's Agent Platform is Changing Everything. Written by Gbemisola Esho, Google Developer Expert (GDE) and Women Techmakers (WTM) Ambassador, the series explains Gemini Enterprise to every level of reader.

This repository contains **Volume 1 — Beginner Level**, designed for anyone who uses a smartphone or email account. No technical knowledge required.
